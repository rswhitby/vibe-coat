About

Vibe Coating is a patchwork of wearable, chroma-key garments that can be worn or unfolded into a shared blanket for gathering. Visitors are invited to submit short “vibe” prompts. Each contribution is gathered to form an atmospheric, continuously evolving collective image that is projected back onto the blue and green-screen fabric. The installation is both medium and display—visualizing the accumulated vibe of its participants as an immersive, continuously changing environment.

August 30 – September 20
